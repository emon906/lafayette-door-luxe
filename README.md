# Lafayette Doorways

Build a premium, bright luxury website for “Garage Doors of Lafayette”.

• Garage Door Installation & Repair

Create a unique high-end garage door website — NOT a generic contractor template and NOT a dark website.

Visual style:
Use a bright warm ivory/cream background with deep navy blue, white and subtle orange/copper accents. Use large clean typography, premium spacing, soft shadows, rounded details and elegant architectural layouts.

Sections:
1. Premium sticky navbar
2. Large hero with garage-door background video, headline and “Get a Free Quote” CTA
3. Trust badges for Local / Reliable / Insured
4. Installation & Repair services with large images
5. Full-width garage door video showcase
6. Residential vs Commercial section with image/video cards
7. Before & After project gallery
8. Visual project showcase mixing photos and short videos
9. Why Choose Us section
10. Full-width video section with CTA overlay
11. Testimonials
12. Final quote/contact section
13. Premium footer

Mix bright images, short videos and editorial layouts throughout the page so it feels dynamic and visually rich.

Videos should autoplay, muted, loop and lazy-load. Add smooth scrolling, subtle reveal animations, premium hover effects and elegant transitions.

Make everything fully responsive, fast and lightweight. Avoid unnecessary libraries and heavy animations.

The final website should feel like an expensive professional garage-door company website with a fresh, bright, modern identity — completely different from typical dark contractor websites.
